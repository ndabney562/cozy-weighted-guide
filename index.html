How to choose

Weight

Heavier isn't always better. Around 4 to 5 lb suits most adults and teens; kids and travel do better with a smaller, lighter plush.

Washing

A removable outer cover is the easiest to keep clean. Many weighted and microwavable plush toys are spot-clean only.

Filling

The weight usually comes from small glass or plastic beads in an inner pouch. Evenly spread weight drapes better than one heavy lump.

Warmth

Some plush, like Warmies, is made to be warmed in a microwave. Only heat one if its label says so.

Not for babies. The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission warns against weighted products for infants, especially during sleep. For young children, follow the age on the label and choose a lighter plush.

Questions people ask

Do weighted stuffed animals help with anxiety?

Many people find the steady pressure calming, the same reason weighted blankets are popular. It's a comfort item, not a treatment; for ongoing anxiety, talk to a doctor.

How heavy should a weighted stuffed animal be?

For adults and teens, around 4 to 5 lb is common. For children, pick a smaller, lighter plush and follow the age on the label.

Can you wash a weighted stuffed animal?

Look for a removable outer cover, like the Hugimals large line. Many weighted and microwavable plush toys are spot-clean only; check the label.

Are weighted stuffed animals safe for kids?

Not for babies. For older children, choose a lighter plush made for their age and follow the label.
